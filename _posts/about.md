At the age of 8, my life changed forever when my father passed away, leaving my mother to raise four children on her own. The strength I witnessed in my mother as she navigated this challenging time taught me invaluable lessons about resilience and determination. Through my journey to sobriety, I found a new relationship with my Higher Power and discovered my passion for technology while reigniting my curiosity about nonprofit work. Because actually, God's the best.

## Current Work & Expertise

At Scorpion Enterprises LLC, I channel these life experiences into everything I do. Scorpion's stack has given me in-depth experience with proprietary CMS systems, React architecture, and .NET integration. Working alongside brilliant engineers across the stack, I support and maintain robust systems that streamline content workflows and drive marketing success across Healthcare, Legal, and Franchise sectors. I develop key features, implement performance optimizations, and ensure seamless connections between front-end interfaces and back-end queries.

Beyond my day-to-day work, I'm deeply invested in the future of AI-assisted development. I've built comprehensive workflows using **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** to bridge AI assistants with development tools, creating autonomous deployment pipelines that integrate JetBrains IDEs, Claude AI, and Cloudflare Workers. This work represents the cutting edge of agentic AI—enabling developers to describe intent and let AI handle implementation, testing, and deployment.

## AI & Integration Expertise

My focus areas include:
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** - Building integrations between AI and development tools
- **Agentic AI Workflows** - Autonomous file creation, deployment automation, and git operations
- **JetBrains IDE Integration** - Configuring MCP servers for seamless AI-assisted development
- **Cloudflare Workers & Pages** - Automated deployments using Wrangler and AI orchestration
- **AI-Enhanced Development** - Using Claude AI Desktop to streamline entire development lifecycles

I share my learnings through technical writing, with articles on Medium covering topics like "JetBrains + Cloudflare + Claude AI: Building Your Own v0.dev" and practical guides to MCP implementation.

## Background & Experience

My tech journey includes roles at Apple and Infosys, providing technical support and solutions to individuals and SMBs. Side projects and volunteering keep me fresh—like leading the One Accord App authentication team at Firm Collective, where I directed cross-collaboration with UI/UX, cybersecurity, and data science teams.

Currently, I'm pursuing an **MBA with Cybersecurity emphasis** at Southern Utah University (AACSB-accredited), while working toward my **AWS AI Practitioner Certification**. My goal is to help protect America's digital resources and advance the responsible development of AI systems.

## Faith & Transformation

My path to sobriety taught me that transformation is possible through faith, determination, and support from others. Today, I combine my technical expertise with a passion for helping others, especially those facing their own challenges. My journey reminds me daily that our greatest struggles can become our greatest strengths—a lesson I first learned watching my mother raise four children on her own.
